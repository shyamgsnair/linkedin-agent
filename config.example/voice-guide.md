# Voice guide

The agent reads this before every draft. Replace each section with how *you* write.

## Who's talking
One or two lines: who you are, and that these are personal views.

## Point of view
What makes your posts yours (e.g. "hands-on: lead with something I built or tried, not predictions").

## Topics
- Topic one
- Topic two

## Tone
e.g. Candid and practical. No hype.

## Shape
- Length: e.g. 50 to 100 words, one idea per post
- Opening: e.g. a sharp contrast line, not a question
- Close: e.g. a practical takeaway

## Formatting
- Hashtags: e.g. 1 to 3 at the end
- Emojis: yes/no

## Never
- Employer branding or speaking for your employer
- Client names or confidential details
- Engagement bait ("Agree?")
- Words to avoid: …

## Reshare commentary
e.g. 1 to 3 sentences that add a concrete example or caveat; don't summarise the original.
